Terms of Service

These terms apply when you request a padel lesson with Coach Navid Fahandezh on coachnavid.com.

Booking requests

Sending a request does not guarantee a lesson. A lesson is booked only once Navid confirms it. You’ll see the confirmation on this site and receive it on WhatsApp. An unanswered request is released after 24 hours.

Changes and cancellations

If you can’t make it, please let Navid know on WhatsApp as early as possible so the slot can go to another player. Navid may also need to move or cancel a lesson; if so, he’ll contact you to find another time.

Prices and payment

Lesson prices and payment are agreed directly with Navid before the lesson.

On court

Lessons take place at the club shown in your booking, and the club’s own rules apply. Padel is a physical sport: take part at your own risk and tell Navid about any injury or health condition before you start.
