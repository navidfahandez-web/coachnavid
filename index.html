Navid has played padel competitively for over twenty years, including a run at World Padel Tour qualifier level in Spain in 2023, and has coached for more than a decade. He has coached the Oman national team, and teaches padel business and coaching internationally as a PadelMBA instructor.

Off court, he is CEO & co-founder of Padel Beast — the community-first club management company behind The Padel Project, Abu Dhabi's highest-occupancy padel facility. Lessons are built around your game: technique, tactics, positioning and match play.
